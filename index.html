Helpful information

Withdrawals

Options vary by country. The office can help with the details for your account.

United States

Funds may be available for withdrawal one day after they are deposited. Ask the office about a debit card and how to load funds onto it.

Israel

Funds may be transferred to a bank account, or you can ask about a card. To send funds to Israel, a request form must be completed each year.

Other Countries

For transfers to other countries, email the main office with your question.
